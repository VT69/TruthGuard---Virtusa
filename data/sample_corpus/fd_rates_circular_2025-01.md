# Fixed Deposit Rates Circular (1 January 2025)

## Interest rates
The fixed deposit interest rate for a 1-year tenor is 6.80% per annum.
The fixed deposit interest rate for a 2-year tenor is 7.00% per annum.

## Senior citizens
Senior citizens receive an additional rate of 0.5% per annum on fixed deposits.

## Premature withdrawal
A premature withdrawal penalty of 1% applies to fixed deposits.
