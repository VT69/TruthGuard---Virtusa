# Board-approved KYC Policy (2027)

## 1. Supersession
This policy supersedes the Board-approved KYC Policy 2024 with effect from 1 January 2027.

## 5. Periodic Updation
Periodic updation of KYC shall be carried out at the following frequency:
- High-risk customers: at least once every twelve months.
- Medium-risk customers: at least once every eight years.
- Low-risk customers: at least once every ten years.

## 6. Video KYC
Customers may complete periodic updation of KYC through Video KYC instead of visiting a branch.
