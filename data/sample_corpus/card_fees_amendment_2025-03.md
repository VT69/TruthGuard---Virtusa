# Amendment No. 1 to the Card Fees and Charges Policy v2

## Amendment to clause 4.2
In partial modification of the Card Fees and Charges Policy v2, clause 4.2 is amended with effect from 15 March 2025.

Where the minimum amount due on a Platinum Credit Card is not paid by the payment due date, a charge of ₹950 shall be levied per statement cycle.

All other clauses of the policy remain unchanged.
