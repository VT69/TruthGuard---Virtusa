# Northwind Banking Glossary

## Minimum amount due
The smallest payment that keeps a credit card account in good standing for a statement cycle.

## Video KYC
A video-based customer identification process conducted by a bank official.

## Provisional credit
A temporary credit given to a customer while a disputed transaction is investigated.
