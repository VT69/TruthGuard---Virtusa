# Personal Loan Policy (v2)

## 3. Eligibility and Terms

### 3.1 Income
Applicants for a personal loan must have a minimum net monthly income of ₹20,000.

### 3.2 Age
The minimum age of a personal loan applicant is 21 years.

### 3.3 Tenure
The maximum repayment tenure of a personal loan is 60 months.

### 3.4 Charges
A processing fee of 2.5% of the loan amount is charged on every personal loan.
