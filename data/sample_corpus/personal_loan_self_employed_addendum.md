# Personal Loan Addendum for Self-Employed Applicants

## Scope
This addendum supplements the Personal Loan Policy v3 for self-employed applicants.

## Income
Self-employed applicants for a personal loan must have a minimum net monthly income of ₹40,000.

## Tenure
For self-employed applicants, the maximum repayment tenure of a personal loan is 48 months.
