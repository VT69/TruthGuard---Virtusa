Help Centre FAQ - Credit Cards

What is the late fee on my Platinum Credit Card?

If the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date, a late fee of ₹500 is charged on the Platinum Credit Card.

What is the annual fee on the Gold Credit Card?

The Gold Credit Card carries an annual fee of ₹1,000.

Is there a mark-up on foreign currency spends with the Platinum Credit Card?

Yes. A foreign currency mark-up of 1.50% applies to the Platinum Credit Card.
