# Card Fees and Charges Policy (v1)

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out the fees and charges that apply to credit cards issued by Northwind Bank.

## 4. Fees and Charges

### 4.1 Annual Fee
- Platinum Credit Card: ₹3,000 per year.
- Gold Credit Card: ₹1,000 per year.

### 4.2 Late Payment Fee
A late payment fee is levied for each statement cycle in which the minimum amount due is not paid by the payment due date.
- Platinum Credit Card: ₹600 per statement cycle.
- Gold Credit Card: ₹500 per statement cycle.
- Classic Credit Card: ₹400 per statement cycle.

### 4.3 Foreign Currency Transactions
A foreign currency mark-up of 1.5% applies to foreign currency transactions on the Platinum Credit Card.
