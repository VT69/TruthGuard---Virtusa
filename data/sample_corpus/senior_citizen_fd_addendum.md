# Senior Citizen Fixed Deposit Addendum

## Scope
This addendum supplements the Fixed Deposit Rates Circular dated 1 January 2025 for senior citizens.

## Premature withdrawal
For senior citizens, the premature withdrawal penalty on fixed deposits is 0.5%.
